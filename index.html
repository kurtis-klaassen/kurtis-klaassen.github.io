About Us

Klaassen Digital is a software business based in Agassiz, British Columbia. We combine years of experience in software, construction and business to build apps that make sense for the people actually using them.

Our first app, TradesClock, came straight out of the trades: a simple way for workers to log their hours and for bosses to see them without paperwork.
